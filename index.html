Addis Ababa

Welcome to Addis Ababa

Addis Ababa, the vibrant capital of Ethiopia, is a city full of history, culture, and modern growth. It is home to more than 5 million people and serves as the political and economic hub of the country. Known as the "Political Capital of Africa," it hosts the headquarters of the African Union and the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa. With a mix of traditional markets, modern skyscrapers, and rich cultural heritage, Addis Ababa offers a unique experience to visitors and locals alike.
